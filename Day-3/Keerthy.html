Keerthy Suresh

Keerthy Suresh is a popular Indian actress known for her work in Tamil, Telugu, and Malayalam films. She was born on 17 October 1992 in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. She began her career as a child artist and later made her lead acting debut in the Malayalam film Geethaanjali (2013). She gained widespread recognition through films such as Mahanati, Nenu Sailaja, Remo, Sarkar, and Dasara.
