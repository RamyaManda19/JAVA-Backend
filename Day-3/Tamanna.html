Tamannaah Bhatia

Tamannaah Bhatia is a popular Indian actress known for her work in Telugu, Tamil, and Hindi films. She was born on 21 December 1989 in Mumbai, Maharashtra. She began her career as a model and made her acting debut in the Hindi film Chand Sa Roshan Chehra (2005). She later gained recognition in South Indian cinema through films such as Happy Days, Kalloori, 100% Love, Racha, and Oosaravelli.
