Kajal Aggarwal

Kajal Aggarwal is a prominent Indian actress known for her work in Telugu, Tamil, and Hindi films. She was born on 19 June 1985 in Mumbai, Maharashtra. She began her career as a model before entering the film industry. Kajal made her acting debut in the Hindi film Kyun! Ho Gaya Na... (2004) and later gained recognition in Telugu cinema with films such as Lakshmi Kalyanam (2007) and Chandamama (2007).
