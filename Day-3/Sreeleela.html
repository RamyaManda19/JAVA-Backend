Sreeleela

Sreeleela is a popular Indian actress and dancer known for her work in Telugu and Kannada films. She was born on 14 June 2001 in Detroit, Michigan, USA, and was raised in Bengaluru, Karnataka. She began her acting career with the Kannada film Kiss (2019), which brought her recognition. She later made her debut in Telugu cinema with Pelli SandaD (2021).
